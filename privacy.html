Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RepKit LLC collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the RepKit mobile application, the RepKit web app at app.repkitapp.com, and our website at repkitapp.com (together, the "App"). By using RepKit, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: name, email address and password (or sign-in through Google or Apple)
  • Meeting and account content: meeting details, pre-call and post-call notes, customer and contact names, and other content you enter into the App
  • Pipeline and compensation information: deals, deal values, quota, commission rate and commission amounts
  • Career information: job history, quota attainment, W2 earnings, key projects and awards, job opportunities, and any screenshots you upload as evidence
  • Your selling profile and memory: what you sell, who you sell to, how you sell, the quotes and philosophy you save, and what RepKit learns from your notes in order to tailor its help to you
  • Daily habits, to-dos, and progress tracking data
  • The companies, industries, and people you ask RepKit to watch or follow
  • AI requests: the questions and prompts you submit, and any screenshots or photos you send to the AI features
  • Voice input: if you dictate, your device's speech recognition (provided by Apple or Google) converts your speech to text. RepKit receives the text, not an audio recording.
  • Support requests and feedback you send us

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • App usage data: features used, screens visited, session activity
  • Device information: device type, operating system version, app version, and time zone
  • Push notification tokens and notification settings
  • Crash reports, error reports, and performance data
  • Analytics events linked to your account identifier (for example, that a meeting was prepped or a trial was started)

1.3 Subscription and Billing Information

Purchases made in the mobile apps are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Purchases made on the web are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of your subscription status but do not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription status across platforms is managed through RevenueCat.

1.4 Information From Public Sources

When you use meeting prep, Targeting, Following, or Find roles, RepKit searches public web sources (for example company news, public posts by the people you follow, and public job listings) and brings the results into the App. This is information about the companies and people you name, not about you.

1.5 Website Visitors

Our website repkitapp.com uses cookies and similar technologies from Google Analytics and from advertising platforms (Google Ads, Meta, Reddit, and X) to measure visits and the performance of our advertising, such as whether a visitor went on to an app store. These tools may collect your IP address, device and browser information, and the pages you view. You can limit this through your browser settings, content-blocking tools, or the opt-out controls those platforms provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features
  • Generate AI output for you, such as meeting prep, answers, weekly briefs, role suggestions, and recaps, tailored to your notes and selling profile
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • Process and verify your subscription status
  • Send you notifications and emails about your account and your work in RepKit (for example meeting reminders, a weekly recap, and billing notices)
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., Quote of the Day, daily habits)
  • Respond to your support requests or communications
  • Monitor for and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve product features and performance
  • Measure the effectiveness of our own advertising
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not show advertising inside RepKit, and we do not use the content you enter (your notes, pipeline, career, or selling information) for advertising. We do use limited event data, such as an app install, a trial start, or a purchase, to measure how our own advertising performs.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

We share data with third-party service providers who help us operate the App, as necessary to provide the service:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud (database, file storage, authentication, server functions, and analytics) — cloud storage and infrastructure
  • Anthropic — AI processing. When you use AI features, the relevant content (such as your notes, selling profile, pipeline and career details, and any screenshots you send) is sent to Anthropic to generate a response.
  • Tavily — web search for research features. Search queries may include company names, contact names, the people you follow, and what you sell.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and entitlement verification
  • Apple, Google, and Stripe — payment processing
  • OneSignal — push notifications
  • Resend — email delivery
  • Sentry — error and crash monitoring
  • Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta, Reddit, and X — website analytics and advertising measurement, as described in Section 1.5

Providers that process your content on our behalf do so in order to deliver the service. Analytics and advertising platforms operate under their own privacy policies.

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or others.

3.3 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change via email or a prominent notice in the App.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers located in the United States. By using the App, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data in the United States.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. AI conversation history, AI usage logs, and analytics event logs are automatically deleted about 180 days after they are created.

If you delete your account, we delete your account and the content you created from our database and file storage. This normally happens right away and in any case within 30 days, except where we are required to retain information for legal or compliance purposes. Some service providers keep limited records under their own retention policies, for example purchase records held by Apple, Google, Stripe, and RevenueCat, and delivery or error logs held by our email, notification, and monitoring providers.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase Security Rules restricting data access to authenticated users
  • Secure credential storage using device keychain/secure storage

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Correction

You may access and update your account information at any time through the account settings in the mobile app or the web app.

6.2 Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the mobile app's account settings by choosing Delete Account. You may also request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at support@repkitapp.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it where you bought it.

6.3 Emails and Notifications

Our emails include an unsubscribe link. You can turn push notifications off in your device settings. We may still send messages that are necessary for your account, such as billing notices.

6.4 California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: (1) know what personal information we collect and how it is used; (2) request deletion of your personal information; (3) opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information); and (4) not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@repkitapp.com.

6.5 European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, or obtain a copy of your personal data. You also have the right to object to processing and to data portability. Our legal basis for processing is your consent (given at account creation) and the performance of our contract with you. To exercise your rights, contact us at support@repkitapp.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

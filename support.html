Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes daily habits and to-dos, pre- and post-meeting notes, and the Philosophy and Career hubs, plus a limited amount of AI use so you can try meeting prep and chat. RepKit Pro unlocks the rest, and both Pro plans start with a 7-day free trial.

Is there a web app?

Yes. Sign in at app.repkitapp.com with the same account you use on your phone. Your data stays in sync between the two.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel where you bought it. On iPhone: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > RepKit. On Android: Google Play Store > Subscriptions > RepKit. If you bought on the web: open Settings in the web app and use the "Bought on the web" billing link. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I request a refund?

For a purchase made on iPhone or Android, refunds are handled by Apple or Google. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com (iOS) or play.google.com/store/account (Android) to submit a refund request. For a purchase made on the web, email support@repkitapp.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the mobile app, open your account settings and choose Delete Account. This removes your account and the content you created, and it cannot be undone. Cancel any active subscription first. You can also request deletion by emailing support@repkitapp.com, and we will process it within 30 days.

How do I stop emails or notifications?

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Push notifications can be turned off for RepKit in your device settings.

Is my data secure?

Yes. RepKit uses Google Firebase with encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and security rules that restrict access to authenticated users only. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What AI model does RepKit use?

RepKit's AI features run on Claude models from Anthropic. When you use an AI feature, the relevant content is sent to Anthropic to generate the response. AI-generated content is for assistance only and should be verified independently.
